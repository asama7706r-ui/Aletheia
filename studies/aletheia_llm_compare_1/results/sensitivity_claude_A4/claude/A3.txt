**A-13.** The laws are the sl₂ relations plus h(h−2)(h+2)=0, and h must be nonzero.

- **graded.** There are no odd generators, so this level does not exist.
- **assoc.** The 3‑dimensional adjoint representation of sl₂ works. There h has eigenvalues 2, 0, −2, so h(h−2)(h+2) = 0 and h ≠ 0.
- **Result.** assoc is the top level, and the freest algebra keeps every live mention nonzero. So the answer is DETERMINED, assoc.

**A-14.** The laws are a² = a+1, b² = b+1 and aba = bab.

- **Mentions.** Every mention (a, b, a·a, a+1, b·b, b+1, a·b·a, b·a·b) is invertible, because a(a−1) = 1 and b(b−1) = 1. So each is nonzero in every admissible algebra.

- **assoc.** The nilCoxeter algebra of S₃ has basis 1, u, v, uv, vu, uvu with uvu ≠ 0, so assoc is viable.

**A-16.** Comparing coefficients in s and t (order a, b, c is preserved) gives a+b+c = 0, ab+ac+bc = −2 and abc = −1.

- **Mentions.** Since abc = −1, none of a, b, c can be zero. The sums t + a·s (and the others) and the full product are nonzero because of the t terms. a+b+c is zero by law, so it is not live. Every admissible algebra therefore keeps all live mentions nonzero.

**A-17.** The laws are u² = u+1, un = n(1−u) and n² = 0.
