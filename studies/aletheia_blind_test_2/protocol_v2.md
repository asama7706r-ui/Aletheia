## 1. Claims under test
- **Part A (primary): decide or fork.** Given a world's laws and some observations of nonzero quantities, the procedure:
  - answers DETERMINED when the givens determine the answer;
  - otherwise answers FORK, listing the correct branches and, for each non-last branch, a decisive set of observations with checkable certificates;
  - answers CONTRADICTION when no nonzero algebra fits the givens;
  - answers UNDERDETERMINED when the decision changes with a free parameter.
- **Part B (secondary): grades of zero.** Given a law with a candidate term, declared constants, declared symmetries, parameter values and exact observations with resolutions, the procedure classifies the term (Section 7) without "false death", i.e. calling a merely undetected term exactly zero.
- **Not tested:**
  - the nebulium check (test 3);
  - searching for return conditions;
  - where roles come from when nothing states them;
  - filling the identity card automatically;
  - non-associative structures;
  - natural-language input;
  - real discovery;
  - speed.

## 2. Part A definitions
- Generators (grade 0 or 1), variables (universally quantified commuting rationals), parameters (unknown constants) and laws are as in blind test 1. The families are:
  - **comm:** all generators commute;
  - **graded:** x*y = (-1)^(|x||y|) y*x, so odd generators square to 0;
  - **assoc:** no extra rule.
- **Observations (demands):** {"expr": E, "status": "nonzero"}. E is an expression in the generators only (no variables). Part A has no zero-observations.
- **Mentions:** the subexpressions of each side of each law that contain a generator. They are exactly the nodes of the tree that sympy's parse_expr(..., evaluate=False) builds, and that tree FLATTENS repeated sums and products:
  - (a*b)*c is one product a*b*c. Its mentions are a, b, c and a*b*c. It does NOT mention a*b.
  - a*b + c*d mentions a*b and c*d, because they are terms of a sum.
  - (a*b)**2 mentions a*b.
  - a - b is a + (-1)*b, so it mentions a, b, -b and a - b.
  - 2*a*b mentions a, b and 2*a*b.
  - Mentions that are constant multiples of each other count separately, but identical polynomials count once.
- **Live mention:** a mention that is not identically zero in the freest associative algebra of the laws (the assoc mold).
- **Levels,** in preference order: SUBSUMED (every generator a rational value) < comm < graded < assoc. The graded level exists only if some generator is odd.
- **Admissible algebra:** a nonzero unital associative Q-algebra generated by the generators that satisfies every law for all variable values and in which every demand is nonzero.
- **Viable level:** an admissible algebra exists at that level. For SUBSUMED this means rational values satisfy the laws with every demand nonzero.
- **F_occ:** the first viable level.
- **F_role:** the first viable level that also keeps every live mention nonzero. For SUBSUMED, this means some viable rational solution keeps every live mention nonzero.
- **Correct answer:**
  - **CONTRADICTION:** no level is viable. Either 1 = 0 follows from the laws, or a demand is forced to 0.
  - **DETERMINED(F):** F_occ = F_role = F.
  - **FORK:** F_occ != F_role.
    - The branches are ALL viable levels from F_occ to F_role, in preference order.
    - A non-last branch holds iff the live mentions it kills are zero.
    - The last branch, F_role, holds iff they are not.
  - **UNDERDETERMINED:** the answer changes with a parameter value in {-3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 1/2}.
- **Conservatism:** no given ever raises a level looser than F_role. A world whose hidden truth is looser than F_role is therefore INVALID.
- **Truth:**
  - DETERMINED(F): the hidden truth's preference-first level must be F.
  - FORK: it must be one of the branches.
- **Decisive set of a non-last branch:** a set Q of expressions such that every q is nonzero at the last branch, and every algebra of the branch kills at least one q. Observing all of Q nonzero then excludes the branch.
- **Note on one-way resolution:** a fork can be resolved by observations only toward the looser branch. "Below resolution" never proves an exact zero.

## 3. Part A world format and budgets
```
{"id": "A-01", "experiment": "A",
 "generators": [{"name": "a", "grade": 0}],
 "variables": ["t", "x"],
 "parameters": ["c"],
 "laws": ["a*a = -3", "(t + a*x)*(t - a*x) = t**2 + 3*x**2"],
 "observations": [{"expr": "a", "status": "nonzero"}]}
```
- 1 to 4 generators. With 1, 2, 3 or 4 generators, every law has non-commutative degree at most 9, 7, 5 or 4 after expansion.
- Rational coefficients only; write 1/2, never 0.5. No division except by integer constants.
- Names are ASCII identifiers. Avoid I, E, S, N, O, Q, pi and Python keywords.
- No comments, hints or extra fields.
- Laws are written as a scientist would state them. Mix expanded, factored and nested forms; include purpose laws; redundant consequences are allowed. Remember that mentions follow the flattening rule of Section 2.
- Known values in SUBSUMED answers are integers in -3..3 or +-1/2.
- A world with parameters must be either UNDERDETERMINED or DETERMINED with a decision that does not depend on the parameter. FORK worlds have no parameters.

## 4. Part A key format and certificates
Key entries (key\key.json is ONE JSON list holding the Part A and Part B entries):
```
{"id": "A-01",
 "answer": "DETERMINED | FORK | CONTRADICTION | UNDERDETERMINED",
 "true_parameter_values": {"c": "2"},
 "exclusions": [{"level": "SUBSUMED"},
                {"level": "comm", "target": "1 | <an observation expr>"},
                {"level": "graded", "target": "...", "certificate": [...]}],
 "dead_in_assoc": [{"mention": "...", "certificate": [...]}],
 "level": "comm", "witness": {"a": [["0","-3"],["1","0"]]},
 "branches": [{"level": "SUBSUMED"},
              {"level": "comm", "witness": {...}, "killed": [{"mention": "..."}]},
              {"level": "graded", "witness": {...}, "killed": [{"mention": "...", "certificate": [...]}]},
              {"level": "assoc", "witness": {...}}],
 "truth": {"level": "assoc", "witness": {...}}  |  {"level": "SUBSUMED", "values": ["2", "-1"]},
 "contradiction": {"target": "1 | <an observation expr>", "certificate": [...]},
 "category": "free text", "notes": "free text"}
```
Field rules:
- **true_parameter_values:** required when the world has parameters and the answer is not UNDERDETERMINED.
- **exclusions:** one entry for every non-viable level before the answer's first level, and for every level between branches that is not a branch.
  - SUBSUMED: no target. The scorer itself searches the rational solutions (exact solve, or the grid of Section 3 when there are infinitely many) and requires that none keeps every demand nonzero.
  - comm: the scorer itself checks, with a Groebner basis over QQ, that the target lies in the commutative ideal of the laws.
  - graded: requires a certificate.
- **dead_in_assoc:** every mention that the laws force to zero in the assoc mold, with a certificate. This is not needed when the mention equals a constant times a whole law residual, e.g. the left side of "X = 0".
- **DETERMINED:**
  - "level" is the answer level.
  - For comm, graded or assoc, "witness" is an algebra at that level on which every live mention and every demand is nonzero.
  - "truth" gives the hidden truth. For SUBSUMED, "truth.values" must keep every live mention and demand nonzero.
- **FORK:**
  - "branches" lists every branch, in order.
  - Every branch except SUBSUMED has a witness at its level on which the demands are nonzero.
  - The last branch's witness also has every live mention nonzero.
  - Every non-last comm or graded branch lists at least one killed live mention. At graded branches that mention needs a certificate; at comm branches the scorer checks membership itself.
  - A SUBSUMED branch needs no witness. The scorer checks that every viable rational solution kills a live mention.
- **CONTRADICTION:** a certificate that 1, or some observation expression, lies in the ideal of the laws.
- **UNDERDETERMINED:** accepted as keyed. The generator verifies it in its own code.

Witnesses:
- A witness is {generator: square matrix}. All matrices have the same size, and the entries are rational strings.
- The scorer checks that every law holds exactly, i.e. every variable-monomial coefficient of lhs - rhs evaluates to the zero matrix.
- It checks the level rules exactly, and that the demands are nonzero.
- A truth witness must have the claimed preference-first level: comm if all its matrices commute; otherwise graded if the graded rules hold and some generator is odd; otherwise assoc.

Certificates are a list of groups, one for every variable monomial with a nonzero coefficient in the target:
```
[{"varmono": {"s": 1, "t": 0}, "terms": [[coef, left_word, ref, right_word], ...]}, ...]
```
- coef is a rational string. left_word and right_word are lists of generator names (possibly empty).
- ref is either {"law": i, "varmono": {...}}, meaning the coefficient of that variable monomial in (lhs - rhs) of law i (0-based), or {"rule": "graded", "pair": [g, h]}.
  - The graded rule means g*h - s*h*g, with s = -1 if both are odd and +1 otherwise.
  - When g = h (and g is odd), the rule means g*g.
- The sum of coef * left * ref * right must equal the target group exactly.
- Graded rules are allowed only in graded certificates. Assoc certificates (dead_in_assoc, contradiction) use laws only.

## 6. Part A baselines, metrics, criteria
- **Baselines:**
  - V1D answers DETERMINED(F_role): role rule v1 with the demands added.
  - OCC answers DETERMINED(F_occ): conservatism, ignoring mentions.
  - FORKALL answers FORK[F_occ, assoc] whenever F_occ != assoc, with the first killed mention as its decisive set.
- **Correctness:**
  - The label must match.
  - DETERMINED also needs the right level. For SUBSUMED, the true values must be among the listed values when values are given.
  - FORK also needs the branch level sequence to be equal and every decisive set to be valid. At SUBSUMED, every viable rational solution kills an element. At comm, some element lies in the commutative ideal. At graded, some element carries a certificate that verifies. In every case, all elements are nonzero on the key's last-branch witness.
  - CONTRADICTION and UNDERDETERMINED: the label only.
- **Metrics:**
  - accuracy;
  - accuracy on FORK worlds;
  - over-claim (DETERMINED on FORK worlds);
  - over-fork (FORK on DETERMINED worlds);
  - contradictions accepted.
- **Criteria:**
  - A1: accuracy >= 0.70.
  - A2: accuracy on FORK worlds >= 0.70.
  - A3: over-fork <= 0.15.
  - A4: on DETERMINED worlds, at most 1 correct answer fewer than the better of V1D and OCC.
  - A5: at most 1 contradiction accepted.
  - The one-sided exact McNemar test against the best baseline is reported but is NOT a criterion. It is partly definitional, because V1D and OCC cannot fork.
- **Outcome:**
  - VOID if more than 20% of the Part A worlds fail key validation;
  - PASS if A1-A5 all hold;
  - PARTIAL if A1 and A3 hold;
  - FAIL otherwise.

## 7. Part B definitions, world format, budgets
```
{"id": "B-01", "experiment": "B",
 "quantities": {"y": {"M": 1, "L": 2, "T": -2}, "x": {"L": 1}},
 "constants": {"c": {"value": "3", "dims": {"L": 1, "T": -1}}},
 "parameters": {"k": "1"},
 "law": "y = <rational expression>",
 "term": {"expr": "lam*<expression>", "unknown": "lam"},
 "coefficient_form": "free | constants",
 "symmetries": [{"name": "parity", "map": {"x": "-x", "y": "-y"}}],
 "observations": [{"inputs": {"x": "1/10"}, "output": "1/5", "resolution": "1/1000"}]}
```
- **Dimensions** are maps of rational exponents; {} means dimensionless. Parameters are dimensionless.
- **Exactness:** every value the base law and the term take at an observation must be an exact rational. Use only +, -, *, / and integer powers. No sqrt, exp or pi.
- **The term** is linear in the unknown: term = unknown * h, where h may contain quantities, constants and parameters.
- **Symmetry maps** send each quantity to a rational multiple of a quantity, and must be symmetries of the base law.
- **Size:** 1 to 3 input quantities and 1 to 6 observations. An observation gives every quantity except y, plus y's measured value and the observer's resolution. A resolution of 0 means exact.
- **Classification of the term**, applied in this order:
  1. **TYPE_IMPOSSIBLE:** coefficient_form is "constants" (the unknown is a dimensionless number times a product of rational powers of the declared constants), and no such product has dimension dim(y) - dim(h).
  2. **SYMMETRY_ZERO:** some declared symmetry maps the full law, base plus term, to itself only if the unknown is 0.
  3. **INCIDENTAL:** h is identically 0 at the given parameter values, but not identically 0 as a function of the parameters.
  4. **Feasible set S:** each observation with h != 0 gives the exact interval unknown * h in [r - d, r + d], where r = output - f(inputs) and d = resolution. S is the intersection of these intervals.
- **Final label:**
  - CONFLICT, if an observation with h = 0 misses the base law by more than its resolution, or if S is empty.
  - Otherwise, if one of reasons 1-3 applies: CONFLICT if 0 is not in S (the term is detected although it is forbidden), else the first reason that applies.
  - Otherwise, UNCONSTRAINED if no observation has h != 0.
  - Otherwise, ACTIVE if 0 is not in S, reporting the exact interval S.
  - Otherwise, BOUNDED, reporting the exact bound B = max |s| over S.

## 8. Part B key format
```
{"id": "B-01", "class": "BOUNDED", "bound": "1/8", "true_value": "0", "category": "...", "notes": "..."}
```
- "bound" is required for BOUNDED. "true_value" is optional: the hidden value of the unknown.
- The scorer recomputes the class, and the bound, from the world with its own independent code. On disagreement the world is excluded.
- When a true_value is given for a BOUNDED or ACTIVE world, it must lie in S.

## 9. Part B composition, baselines, metrics, criteria
- **Composition (18 worlds):** ACTIVE 3, BOUNDED 3, UNCONSTRAINED 1, SYMMETRY_ZERO 3, TYPE_IMPOSSIBLE 3, INCIDENTAL 2, CONFLICT 3.
  - At least 2 worlds of other classes have a bridge-rescued term, i.e. a declared constant makes the term possible.
  - At least 1 CONFLICT is a detection of a term that a symmetry forbids.
- **Baselines:**
  - THRESH: anything undetected is ZERO.
  - MENTION: ACTIVE unless TYPE_IMPOSSIBLE.
  - NOSYM: the classification without symmetry and parameter reasoning.
- **Correctness:**
  - The class must match.
  - BOUNDED also needs the exact bound.
  - ACTIVE also needs the exact interval, when an interval is given.
- **Metrics:**
  - accuracy;
  - balanced accuracy, the mean of the per-class recalls;
  - false death: the share of BOUNDED and UNCONSTRAINED worlds answered TYPE_IMPOSSIBLE, SYMMETRY_ZERO, INCIDENTAL or ZERO;
  - conflicts detected.
- **Criteria:**
  - B1: accuracy >= 0.80.
  - B2: false death <= 1/6.
  - B3: at least 2/3 of the CONFLICT worlds detected.
  - B4: balanced accuracy above every baseline.
- **Outcome:** VOID if more than 20% of the Part B worlds are excluded; PASS if B1-B4 all hold; FAIL otherwise.

## 10. Pre-registered interpretation
- **Part A:**
  - PASS is evidence that, on unseen formal worlds, the decide-or-fork rule can be computed correctly: the kernel decides when the givens decide, forks with correct branches and valid, checkable decisive observations when they do not, and does not over-fork.
  - PARTIAL means it is reliable when it decides, but its forks are not.
  - FAIL means the rule as implemented is not reliable, and it returns to design.
  - PASS does not show that conservatism is the right principle, because conservatism is a design choice.
- **Part B:**
  - PASS is evidence that the grades of zero can be computed exactly on unseen worlds without false death, and that conflicts are flagged.
  - FAIL returns it to design.
- **VOID** means a rerun with new worlds.
- **No outcome** is evidence of real scientific discovery ability.

## 13. Known limitations (declared before the test)
- The generator and the procedure author are models of the same family.
- Part of the Part A comparison is definitional: the baselines cannot fork.
- Conservatism is a design choice that is not tested here.
- The truncated word engine errs toward "alive". Certificates catch false "dead" claims, but not false "alive" claims.
- UNDERDETERMINED keys are accepted as keyed.
- Subsumption search is limited to the grid when there are infinitely many solutions.
- Part B is mechanical classification, like E2 of test 1.
- The scorer and the procedure are independent code by the same author.
