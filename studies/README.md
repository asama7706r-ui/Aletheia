Each folder is one self-contained study with all of its materials: protocol, procedure, scorer, worlds, key, raw outputs, report, and audit. Folder names are kept exactly as they were during the studies, because some sealed scripts locate their sibling folders by these names.

## How the studies protect themselves
1. **Seal first.** The procedure and the scorer are frozen, and their SHA-256 hashes are published before any test world exists.
2. **Separate generator.** Worlds and the answer key are generated by a separate session that cannot read the procedure. Every key claim carries a machine-checkable witness or certificate.
3. **Hash order.** The key hash is published. The frozen procedure runs. Its outputs hash is published before the key is opened. Then the frozen scorer runs, and its report is published verbatim.
4. **Audits are separate.** Post-hoc audits are labelled exploratory and never change an outcome.
5. **Sealed files are never edited.** A change means a new study.
